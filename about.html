About this project

Practical information for a new city.

Istanbul Student Guide is an independent starter project designed to help students and newcomers prepare for life in Istanbul.

Our approach

We aim to make everyday topics easier to understand with checklists and plain-language explainers. We are not a government body, university, transport operator, estate agent, or legal adviser.
