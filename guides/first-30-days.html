Deadlines to know from day one

WhatDeadlineGuide
University final registrationThe dates on your admission letter; missing them can cost you your placeRegistration
Residence permit applicationWithin your legal stay (before your visa or visa-exemption period ends)Residence permit
General Health Insurance (GSS)Within three months (apply early: sources count from registration or from the start of the academic year)Registrations
Phone bought abroadUsable unregistered for 120 days from entryRegistrations

Practical advice

  • Recommendation Keep one folder (paper and digital) with your passport copy, visa, admission letter, student certificate, permit application, receipts, lease, and insurance.
  • Recommendation Use only official websites and offices. These applications and registrations do not require paid agents.
  • Recommendation Ask your international student office early. They see the same questions every term and know your university's process.
  • Recommendation Rules and fees change. Check the "last verified" date on each guide and the official source before you pay or sign.
