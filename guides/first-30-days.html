Save these numbers

Save these in your phone before you need them.

  • 112EmergencyPolice, ambulance, fire, and other emergencies. Türkiye's single emergency number.
  • 157YİMERForeigners Communication Centre: residence permits and immigration. Line 2 is for international students, weekdays 08:00–18:00. From abroad: +90 312 157 11 22.
  • 153Beyaz MasaIstanbul Metropolitan Municipality's service line, including İstanbulkart questions.

Common mistakes and scams

Mistakes that cause problems later

  • Arriving on an e-Visa or visa exemption when your programme needs a student visa.
  • Waiting until your legal stay is almost over before applying for a residence permit.
  • Missing the three-month window for student General Health Insurance.
  • Forgetting to register your address after the permit card arrives, which causes problems at renewal.
  • Following a friend's document list from a different university or year.
  • Lending your student İstanbulkart: if someone else is caught using it, the card is blocked and you pay a penalty.

Scams to watch for

  • Fake listings and deposit scams. A cheap flat, pressure to pay quickly, and a request for a deposit before you can view it. Verify the owner and any agent's authorisation certificate first. How to check
  • Paid "visa" or "permit agents". Residence permit applications are made only on the official e-İkamet system; no intermediary company is authorised to apply for you.
  • Unofficial taxis and touts at airports and stations. Use official ranks, apps, or public transport.
  • Calls or messages claiming to be police, officials, or your bank and asking you to transfer money or share passwords or codes. Recommended Hang up, do not pay, and contact the organisation through its official number, or 112 in an emergency.
  • Anyone asking to keep your passport or your e-Devlet password. Do not hand them over.
