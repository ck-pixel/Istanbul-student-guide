Student residence permit: how to apply in Istanbul

If you study for a degree in Türkiye and stay longer than your visa allows, you need a student residence permit (öğrenci ikamet izni). This guide explains who needs it, when and how to apply on e-İkamet, what it costs in 2026, and the mistakes that cause delays.

Who needs a student residence permit

Legal requirement Foreigners studying for an associate, bachelor's, master's, or PhD degree at a higher-education institution in Türkiye are issued a student residence permit, and are obliged to hold one (Law No. 6458, Art. 38; Implementing Regulation, Art. 35).

  • Legal requirement You need a residence permit if you will stay longer than your visa or visa-exemption period, or longer than 90 days (Law No. 6458, Art. 19).
  • Legal requirement A student residence permit is not issued for "special student" status, open education, or distance education (Regulation, Art. 35/14).
  • Legal requirement Exchange students who come under an international agreement or exchange programme apply for a short-term residence permit, not a student permit (Law No. 6458, Art. 31).
  • Legal requirement If you already hold a family residence permit or a work permit, you keep it and get the same rights as a student permit holder (Regulation, Art. 26).
  • Legal requirement If your university places you in a Turkish preparatory programme, the permit is issued until the start of the next study term (Regulation, Art. 35/10).

Deadlines

Legal requirement Complete your university enrollment and submit your residence permit application within your legal stay period, meaning before your visa or visa-exemption period ends (Presidency of Migration Management, 2026 student brochure).

Legal requirement The application is made after your pre-registration with the university is complete (Regulation, Art. 36).

Legal requirement The authority must decide on an application within 90 days at the latest (Law No. 6458, Art. 21).

The Regulation (Art. 21) also accepts applications from people whose visa or exemption expired up to 10 days earlier. Recommendation Do not plan around this. Apply as soon as you have pre-registered, because a late application can lead to penalties.

University registration and student status

Your residence permit depends on your status as an enrolled student, so the university is part of the process from the start.

  • Legal requirement Apply for the permit after your pre-registration with the university is complete (Regulation, Art. 36), and complete university enrollment within your legal stay (2026 student brochure). See the university registration guide.
  • Legal requirement Submit the documents listed on your application form to your university's international student office, without waiting for your appointment date (2026 how-to leaflet).
  • Legal requirement Changes to your student or enrollment status must be reported within 20 days, and freezing your registration cancels your student permit from that date (Regulation, Art. 35). See changes you must report.
  • Needs confirmation Whether you must also hand in a separate student certificate (öğrenci belgesi) depends on your university's process; official sources are not consistent. Ask your international student office.
  • Recommendation Make sure your name is written exactly the same way in your passport, your university record, and your e-İkamet application.

Step by step: applying on e-İkamet

Legal requirement Applications are made only through the official e-İkamet system. No intermediary company is authorised to apply for you.

  1. Go to e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr and start a pre-registration with the passport you used to enter Türkiye and a digital biometric photo.
  2. Choose the option for a first-time residence permit application.
  3. Complete the Student Residence Permit application. Enter your own phone number and email, and your health-insurance details.
  4. Select the province where your university is located (Istanbul).
  5. Choose "Create appointment request / print registration document" to select your appointment city and date, and print the application form.
  6. Pay the residence permit card fee (see Fees). You can pay online with the "Make Payment" option, at a tax office, or at a contracted bank.
  7. Submit your documents to your university's international student office as soon as possible. The 2026 official leaflet says to do this without waiting for your appointment date.
  8. Watch for SMS messages and check your application result on e-İkamet regularly (see after you apply).

Needs confirmation The official leaflet mentions both an appointment and submitting documents through the university. Ask your university's international student office exactly how applications are handled for your university this term.

Legal requirement Health insurance is mandatory for a student residence permit. Students who apply for Turkish General Health Insurance (GSS) within three months do not need other insurance; if you miss that window, you must buy private health insurance (Regulation, Art. 35/8). Needs confirmation Official sources count the three months differently: the Regulation and SGK count from your first registration at university, while the e-İkamet document list counts from the start of the academic year. Apply as early as you can to be safe.

After you apply: tracking, missing documents, and your card

  • Tracking. Check the status of your application and the PTT tracking code for your card under "I Would Like to See My Application Result" on e-İkamet (2026 brochure).
  • Legal requirement Missing documents. If the result shows "Missing Document", you must complete the listed documents and submit them within the deadline shown (2026 how-to leaflet). You may also be told by SMS. Recommendation Contact your international student office straight away so the documents reach the right office in time.
  • The permit card is sent by PTT (Turkish post) to the address you declared. The card shows your foreigner identification number, which starts with 99.
  • Legal requirement Register your address within 20 working days of receiving your residence permit card (2026 student brochure).
  • Legal requirement A current address registration (a residence certificate, yerleşim yeri belgesi, from e-Devlet) and a utility bill are required when you later apply for an extension.

Changes you must report

  • Legal requirement Report a change of address, marital status, or the name in your passport to the provincial migration office within 20 working days (Regulation, Art. 23).
  • Legal requirement If you move to another province, apply for a residence permit there within 20 working days.
  • Legal requirement Changes in your student or enrollment status must be reported within 20 days (Regulation, Art. 35/11). If you change faculty or university within Istanbul, tell your international student office within 20 days. If you transfer to a university in another province, apply through e-İkamet within 10 days (e-İkamet document list).
  • Legal requirement If you freeze your registration (kayıt dondurma), your student permit is cancelled from that date (Regulation, Art. 35/13).

Renewal and graduation

  • Legal requirement The permit can be issued for the whole study programme. If you do not graduate on time, it can be extended by up to one year at a time (Regulation, Art. 37).
  • Legal requirement A permit cannot run until less than 60 days before your passport expires (Law No. 6458, Art. 23).
  • Legal requirement To extend, apply from 60 days before the permit expires, and in every case before it expires (Regulation, Art. 21).
  • Legal requirement When you graduate, your student permit ends and you have 10 days to apply for a permit with a new purpose. Graduates who apply within six months of graduating may be granted a short-term permit (Law No. 6458, Art. 31).

Overstaying

Legal requirement Staying without a valid visa or permit can lead to a fine and an entry ban. If you overstay by more than 10 days and leave voluntarily after paying the penalty, an entry ban cannot exceed one year (Regulation, Art. 8). The general maximum entry ban is five years (Law No. 6458, Art. 9). The fine depends on nationality, length of overstay, and age.

Needs confirmation We do not list fine amounts, because they are revalued every year and we could not verify the 2026 figures from an official source.

Common mistakes

  • Waiting until the visa is almost expired before enrolling and applying.
  • Paying an agency or "fixer". Applications are made only on e-İkamet, and no intermediary firm is authorised.
  • Entering a friend's or agent's phone number, so you miss SMS requests for extra documents.
  • Submitting a rental contract that is not notarised.
  • Missing the three-month window for student General Health Insurance and then having no valid insurance.
  • Forgetting to register your address after the card arrives, which causes problems at renewal.
  • Ignoring a "Missing Document" result until after the deadline.

Scams to avoid

Legal requirement Residence permit applications are made only at e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr, and no intermediary institution or firm is authorised by the Presidency of Migration Management (2026 how-to leaflet).

  • Recommendation Be suspicious of anyone who promises guaranteed approval, a faster appointment, or "special access" for a fee. Nobody can guarantee the outcome.
  • Recommendation Only enter your details on websites ending in goc.gov.tr. Check the address bar before you log in or pay.
  • Recommendation Pay the card fee only through official channels: e-İkamet's "Make Payment" option, a tax office, or a contracted bank. Never pay an individual.
  • Recommendation Never share your e-Devlet password, and do not hand your passport to anyone other than an official office.
  • Recommendation Do not use a borrowed, fake, or "rented" address or rental contract. Your permit and address registration rely on your real address.

Practical advice

  • Recommendation Keep digital and paper copies of every document and receipt, and the printed application form.
  • Recommendation Check your passport's expiry date before applying. Renewing your passport first can avoid getting a shorter permit.
  • Recommendation Ask your international student office for its exact document list and submission method before you print anything.
  • Recommendation Set calendar reminders for 60 days before your permit expires and 20 working days after any move.

Where to get help

  • Istanbul Provincial Directorate of Migration Management: istanbul.duzenli@goc.gov.tr. Include a photo of your passport and page 1 of your application form.
  • Your university's international student office.
