University registration for international students in Istanbul

After you receive an acceptance letter, you still need to complete final registration (kesin kayıt) at your university. This guide takes you from acceptance letter to registration: the student visa, how to confirm dates and payments, commonly requested documents, diploma equivalence, your student certificate, and how registration connects to your residence permit.

National rules and university rules

Every university and programme sets its own requirements. There is no single national registration checklist: the official Study in Türkiye portal says documents vary by university and programme, because universities determine their own conditions for admitting international students. Nothing on this page is a universal requirement unless it is marked Legal requirement. Always follow your own university's instructions.

Your acceptance or placement letter and your university's announcements are the authoritative sources for your case. For example:

  • University rule Marmara University's 2026–2027 guide states that the placement result document lists the registration dates, the place of registration, and the documents required.
  • University rule Istanbul Technical University's 2026–2027 announcement states that the required registration documents and deadlines will be announced on the university's website.

Before you travel: the student visa

Legal requirement Türkiye's e-Visa is valid only for tourism or commerce. For study, visas are issued by Turkish embassies and consulates (Ministry of Foreign Affairs). Study in Türkiye advises applying as soon as you receive your admission letter.

University rule Istanbul Technical University's 2026–2027 announcement states that a student visa is mandatory both for entry into Türkiye and for registration at ITU.

Needs confirmation Some nationalities may have different visa arrangements. We could not confirm the exceptions from an official page. Ask the Turkish consulate in your country and your university before you travel.

Step by step: from acceptance letter to registration

  1. Read your acceptance letter carefully. Note the registration dates, the place (online or in person), the document list, and any payment instructions.
  2. Check your university's announcements for the official registration document list and deadlines for your programme (see how to confirm the details).
  3. Start diploma equivalence early if your university requires it (see diploma equivalence).
  4. Prepare translations and certifications. Documents not in Turkish or English usually need official translation; some universities and authorities also ask for notarisation or an apostille.
  5. Apply for your student visa at a Turkish embassy or consulate as soon as you have the acceptance letter.
  6. Pay tuition, if your programme charges it, following your university's instructions. ITU, for example, states that registration will not be completed without payment.
  7. Travel to Istanbul before the registration dates, with the original documents and copies.
  8. Complete registration on the announced dates, online or at the student affairs office.
  9. Get your student certificate (öğrenci belgesi) once you are registered.
  10. Start your residence permit application straight after pre-registration, within your legal stay (see how registration connects to your residence permit).

How to confirm the details for your programme

Before you travel, confirm these four things in writing from an official university source: your acceptance letter, the university's announcements page, or an email from the international student office or student affairs office.

  • Legal requirement For MEB equivalence, documents need an apostille, or certification by the issuing country's authorities and the Turkish mission where apostille does not apply. Notarised translations may be requested.
  • Legal requirement YÖK states that foreign applicants must obtain a foreigner identification number from the Ministry of Interior before applying for equivalence.
  • Legal requirement According to YÖK, foreign nationals entering non-clinical postgraduate programmes in Türkiye do not need degree equivalence; they receive a recognition letter for higher-education institutions instead.

Needs confirmation Whether you need equivalence before or after registration depends on your university. Some ask for it at registration, others allow it later. Confirm with your admissions office.

Late registration risks

University rule Missing the registration dates can cost you your place. Marmara University's 2026–2027 guide states that candidates who do not register on the specified dates are considered to have given up their right to register.

University rule ITU's 2026–2027 announcement states that registration will not be completed without payment, and applicants who do not pay lose their registration rights.

  • Registering late also shortens the time you have to apply for your residence permit within your legal stay, and to apply for student health insurance.
  • Recommendation If a visa delay or emergency may make you late, contact the international student office before the deadline, in writing, and ask what options exist. Do not assume a late registration will be accepted.

Your student certificate (öğrenci belgesi)

After registration, you can get a student certificate (öğrenci belgesi) confirming that you are enrolled. You will need it for things like transport discounts, and possibly for your residence permit.

  1. From your university: ask the student affairs office (öğrenci işleri) or use your university's student information system. Ask for a stamped or e-signed version if it is for an official application.
  2. From e-Devlet: e-Devlet lists Council of Higher Education (YÖK) student-certificate services. To use e-Devlet, foreigners need an e-Devlet password, which PTT issues to people with a foreigner identification number (starting with 99) once they have a residence permit card. Until then, use the university route.

Recommendation Request a fresh certificate when an office needs a recent one (İstanbulkart, for example, asks in-person applicants for one issued within the last month), and check that your name matches your passport exactly.

How registration connects to your residence permit

Legal requirement You apply for a student residence permit after your pre-registration with the university is complete (Implementing Regulation of Law No. 6458, Art. 36), and you must complete enrollment and apply within your legal stay period (Presidency of Migration Management, 2026 student brochure).

Registration does not give you residence status. Being registered at a university makes you eligible to apply, but only an approved residence permit allows you to stay longer than your visa. The migration authority decides each application.

  • Your university's international student office usually receives your residence permit documents. See the student residence permit guide for the full process, documents, and the 2026 card fee.
  • Legal requirement Changes in your enrollment must be reported within 20 days, and freezing your registration (kayıt dondurma) cancels your student permit from that date (Implementing Regulation, Art. 35).

Common mistakes

  • Travelling on an e-Visa or visa exemption when your university requires a student visa.
  • Starting translations and equivalence too late, then missing the registration window.
  • Following a friend's document list from a different university, programme, or year.
  • Leaving original documents at home because a copy was accepted for the application.
  • Paying tuition through an unofficial intermediary instead of the method your university specifies.
  • Assuming that registration alone allows you to stay; you still need a residence permit.
  • Leaving health insurance until after registration and missing the General Health Insurance window.

Practical advice for students arriving from abroad

  • Recommendation Email your international student office before you travel to confirm the document list, the registration format, and whether originals are needed.
  • Recommendation Carry originals in your hand luggage, not checked bags, with several copies and scans on your phone and in cloud storage.
  • Recommendation Plan to arrive a few days before registration so a flight delay does not make you miss it.
  • Recommendation Bring extra passport photos and some money for local notarisation or copies.
  • Recommendation Keep the receipt for tuition and every official fee.
  • Recommendation Read the first 30 days checklist for what comes after registration.
