Privacy notice template

Privacy matters.

This site has no accounts, analytics, or advertising scripts. It collects only what you choose to send through the “Was this helpful?” buttons and the suggestion form.

Before launch

This page is a template, not legal advice or a completed compliance assessment. Before adding analytics, advertising, affiliate tools, cookies, forms, or third-party embeds, update this notice to accurately explain what data is collected, by whom, for what purpose, how long it is retained, and how users can exercise applicable rights.

“Was this helpful?” answers

When you click Yes or No at the end of a guide, we store the page name, your answer, and the date and time. We do not store your IP address or any identifier with the answer. Your browser also remembers on your own device that you have already answered, so the buttons stay disabled.

Suggestion form

When you send the suggestion form, we store the type of message, the page it is about, your message, the email address if you choose to give one, and the date and time. We do not store your IP address with the message. We use this only to review and correct the guides, and to reply if you gave an email address. Please do not include passport, residence permit, or other identity numbers.

Where this data is kept

Answers and suggestions are stored in a database provided by our hosting provider, Cloudflare. Checklist ticks in the guides are saved only in your own browser (local storage) and are never sent to us; clearing your browser data removes them.

Publisher task: state how long suggestions and email addresses are kept, and how people can ask for their message to be deleted, before relying on this notice.

Third-party services

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. Document the services you actually enable and review applicable privacy and cookie requirements in Türkiye.
